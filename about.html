About Rubio Express

Built By A Tradesman
Who Started On The Crew.

Founded in 2017 by Rudy Ramirez Rubio, Rubio Fencing LLC is a family-built subcontractor known for reliable crews, dependable schedules, and quality that holds up.

With roughly 1,000 completed projects—from 5,000 to over 200,000 linear feet across commercial and renewable energy work—and a network of family-run contractors to scale on demand, the standard stays the same:

“Do it right. Deliver on time. Earn the next opportunity.”
