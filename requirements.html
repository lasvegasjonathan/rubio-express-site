Working with our crews

Dependability

Arrive on time and communicate changes in your availability.

Safety

Follow jobsite instructions, use required protective equipment, and ask questions when you need guidance.

Teamwork

Work respectfully with your crew and communicate clearly.

Apprenticeship applicants

New to the trade

Ask about the entry requirements for available apprenticeship opportunities. Be prepared to attend classes and learn through supervised job experience.

Tell us about your interest in construction and the skills you want to develop.

Bringing experience

Share your current apprenticeship program, completed classes, relevant credentials, and field experience.

Ask how your previous training may fit available opportunities.

Travel and availability

Some opportunities involve travel. Let us know your availability and ask about the project location, work schedule, and room and board compensation arrangements.

What to have ready

When you're ready to take the next step, be sure to have current contact information, relevant work experience, training or certifications you already hold, and your availability dates to start work and travel.
