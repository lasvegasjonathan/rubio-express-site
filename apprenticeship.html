Apprenticeships at
Rubio Express

Rubio Express LLC welcomes people beginning a construction career and apprentices who already have training and experience. Explore how apprenticeship classes, paid field work, and continued learning can support your next step.

Classes that connect to the jobsite

Apprenticeship classes help you understand the knowledge behind the work. NCCER, the National Center for Construction Education and Research, develops construction training used across the industry.

Its Core curriculum introduces:

  • Jobsite safety
  • Construction math
  • Hand and power tools
  • Construction drawings

Ask our team about the NCCER courses, class schedule, and training pathway available to you.

Put your learning to work

Build experience alongside crews working on commercial fencing and renewable-energy projects. Learn how classroom concepts relate to real tasks, teamwork, and the expectations of an active jobsite.

Competitive wages and travel opportunities with paid room and board support you as you develop your skills.

A foundation for your next step

Training and field experience can help you work toward skilled craft roles and specialized projects. With additional experience and training, future opportunities may include crew leadership or supervision.

When earned, NCCER credentials provide a portable record of completed training that employers can verify.

Career paths depend on your trade, completed training, experience, and the requirements of each role.
